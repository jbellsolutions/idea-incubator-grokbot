# Incubator: one agent per role

Generated by `python3 tools/agents.py`. Each role is its own Grok bot, its own agent. They are all on the same Grok account, so they share one computer, one workspace (`/workspace/idea-incubator/`) and its sign-ins. The lead agent plans the day, keeps the scoreboard and holds the one queue for the owner. Agents hand work to each other through files in `requests/<role>/`.

| Role | The bot to make | Job | Its page |
|---|---|---|---|
| lead | **Incubator · lead** | The orchestrator: plans the day from the worker's digest, turns Justin's ideas into desk orders, routes results, posts the scoreboard, writes the vault daily note and keeps Justin's queue. It does no heavy work itself. | [agents/lead.md](agents/lead.md) |
| markets | **Incubator · markets** | The one market job only Grok can do cheaply: search X for coins and narratives. Everything else in markets (signals, coin checks, creator-follow, backtests, the paper book) runs on the worker; this role hands candidates over, reads the worker's numbers and files trade tickets for Justin. | [agents/markets.md](agents/markets.md) |
| promoter | **Incubator · promoter** | Turns each finished product into a Gumroad listing and posts on Justin's own X, drafts every one, and publishes only what Justin approved. Sales are read by the worker; this role reports them. | [agents/promoter.md](agents/promoter.md) |

## Setting the team up

1. Make the lead bot and paste the pack's one link into it (HANDOFF.md, Part 1). It unpacks the pack into `/workspace/idea-incubator/repo/` and builds the workspace.
2. For each other role, make one bot with the name above and paste the line on its page. It reads its own page from the shared workspace and sets itself up.
3. Every routine is made on its own role's bot, switched off.

3 agents. A bot holds 50 routines, so each agent has its own 50.
